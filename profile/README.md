## 🎯 About This Organization & Mission

Welcome to **MDRG-MODS** — an independent, open, community-driven preservation archive for mods compatible with **!Ω Factorial Omega: My Dystopian Robot Girlfriend (MDRG)**.

### Why this archive exists
Most community mods for MDRG were originally distributed solely through the official Discord server. However, accessing those channels has become increasingly restrictive and intrusive due to third-party age verification systems demanding government IDs and sensitive personal documents. 

We believe that **game modding, Lua scripts, and cosmetic enhancements should remain accessible to players without compromising personal identity and privacy.**

### Core Principles
1. **Original Code Preserved (100% Untouched):** None of the underlying Lua scripts, mod manifests, or visual assets have been modified. They are preserved in their exact original form as published by their creators.
2. **Full Attribution & Credits:** Every repository acknowledges the original modder with Discord handles, IDs, and links where known.
3. **Community Compatibility:** Sourced directly from official Discord mod releases for MDRG build **v0.97.12**.
4. **Direct, Fast Downloads:** Ready-to-use `.zip` packages are attached to each repository's GitHub Releases tab.
5. **Open Collaboration:** Any modder or contributor can join the organization or submit their work directly.

---

## ⚙️ Internal vs. External Mods

All mods archived in this organization are **Internal Mods**:
- They rely entirely on MDRG's built-in native in-game mod system (mod editor / importer / manager) available across **PC, Mac, and Android**.
- They do **not** require any third-party external mod loaders (such as LemonLoader, MelonLoader, or BepInEx).
- Ready-to-use mod archives are available under the **Releases** tab of each repository.

## 🛡️ Content Policy & GitHub Compliance

- **No Sexually Explicit Media:** All repositories in this organization host source code (Lua scripts, JSON metadata), audio clips, and cosmetic overlay textures. **No pornographic imagery, explicit video, or graphic nudity is hosted on these repositories.**
- **Non-Commercial & Educational Preservation:** This archive is maintained purely for archival, educational, and interoperability purposes under fair use.
- **Takedowns & Inquiries:** If you are an original creator and wish to claim your repository, update your credits, or request repository removal, please open an Issue or contact the organization maintainer.
